EO1

Copies EO1bian to the EO1's own memory: faster start, and you can remove the stick.
It cannot brick the EO1: the part that starts it is never written, and it always starts from a USB stick first, so this stick brings it back at any time. Android is kept on the EO1 (only its old data area is reused, like a factory reset), and your old Electric Objects art is copied to the stick first.

Use your EO1 away from home too
This page only works on your home Wi-Fi. The live remote shows what this EO1 shows and changes pictures, brightness and the screen from anywhere, on mobile data too: no account, no app, free. It goes through ntfy.zap.pm, a free ntfy server for EO1bian frames with no daily limit, encrypted end to end, so it only carries scrambled bytes.
